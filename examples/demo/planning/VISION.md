## Problem

- Small fitness and craft studios take bookings by message and spreadsheet, so classes double-book and no-shows go unpaid.

## Customer

- Owners of studios with one to three rooms and up to 400 regular clients, who pay a monthly fee.

## v1 is done when

<!-- Observable outcomes. Each roadmap item must serve at least one line. -->

- V1: A client books a class from the studio's page in under a minute, and the slot can no longer be double-booked.
- V2: A client pays at booking, and the studio sees the payment in its dashboard the same day.
- V3: Every booking sends a confirmation email in the studio's own timezone.
- V4: A studio owner sets up a new studio without help in under 30 minutes.

## Out of scope for v1

<!-- Anything here is parked automatically, without discussion. -->

- Native mobile apps.
- Marketplaces that list many studios together.

## Constraints

- Time available: 20 hours per week
- Budget: 150 EUR per month for hosting and tools
- Stack: TypeScript, Postgres, one hosted payment provider
