# ADR-002 Pin the Astro major

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-01
- Category: architecture
- Supersedes: ADR-001
- Item: R-007
- Source: none

## Context

The billing pages for R-007 render with Astro, and a new major broke the build.

## Decision

Pin the Astro major in `package.json` and upgrade it as its own item.

None recorded.

## Consequences

None recorded.
